## Proposed list: GB-OPCC — UK Police & Crime Commissioners / OPCCs

### Proposed list code
`GB-OPCC` — jurisdiction `GB` (ISO 3166, the United Kingdom incl. Northern Ireland) + list-name code `OPCC` (the real acronym; not `OPC`).

### Name
UK Police & Crime Commissioners / OPCCs (RCVDA register)

### Managed by
Redcar & Cleveland Voluntary Development Agency (RCVDA), a UK charity (Charity No. 1135959). Published openly on GitHub.

### Description
Open identifiers for UK police-governance bodies — Offices of the Police and Crime Commissioner (OPCCs) and their equivalents. A `GB-OPCC` code is minted **only** where the body has no usable existing identifier (standalone OPCC corporations sole, MOPAC in London, and the Scottish Police Authority). Where the PCC function sits with a body that already has an identifier — a combined authority, the City of London Corporation, the NI Policing Board, or a police authority already in the government-organisation register — that body is **referenced**, not minted.

### Geographic coverage
GB (the United Kingdom, including Northern Ireland).

### Legal structure
Statutory body / corporation sole.

### Sector
Policing governance.

### List type
Third-party.

### Information about the list
https://github.com/rcvda/opcc-names-and-codes

### Looking up identifiers / open-data copy
Open CSV: https://github.com/rcvda/opcc-names-and-codes/blob/main/data/register.csv — look up the body by name/area; the `org_id` column holds the `GB-OPCC` code. Archived with a DOI: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24063722

### Example identifier (verified)
`GB-OPCC-00005` — Office of the Police and Crime Commissioner for Cleveland. Minted by RCVDA; paired to the force `GB-POL-00006`. A corporation sole with no charity/company/government-organisation identifier — a genuine gap.

### Relevance
Used for linking UK voluntary-sector and public-sector organisation data, in the same ecosystem as Find That Charity and 360Giving. Sibling to the UK police forces register (proposed `GB-POL`).
