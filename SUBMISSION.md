# org-id.guide submission — GB-OPCC

Draft PROPOSAL for adding the `GB-OPCC` list to org-id.guide. Submit as a PROPOSAL issue (or PR) on the org-id.guide register on GitHub, under a GitHub account, once this repo is public.

## Metadata

- **List code:** `GB-OPCC`
- **Name:** UK Police & Crime Commissioners / OPCCs (RCVDA register)
- **Description:** Canonical identifiers for UK police-governance bodies (OPCCs and equivalents). RCVDA mints a code where the body has no usable existing identifier; where the PCC function sits with an already-identifiable body (a combined authority, the City of London Corporation, etc.) that body is referenced instead.
- **Geographic coverage:** GB (ISO 3166 — the United Kingdom, incl. Northern Ireland)
- **Legal structure:** Statutory body / corporation sole
- **Sector:** Policing governance
- **List type:** Third-party
- **Available online:** https://github.com/rcvda/opcc-names-and-codes (data: `data/register.csv`)
- **How to locate identifiers:** Look up the body by name/area in `data/register.csv`; the `org_id` column holds the `GB-OPCC` code.
- **Example identifiers:** `GB-OPCC-00005` (Office of the Police and Crime Commissioner for Cleveland), `GB-OPCC-00001` (Avon and Somerset)
- **Open licence:** yes — Open Government Licence v3.0
- **Last updated:** 2026-11-08

## Verified example

`GB-OPCC-00005` — Office of the Police and Crime Commissioner for Cleveland. Minted by RCVDA; paired to `GB-POL-00006`. A corporation sole with no charity/company/GB-GOR identifier — a genuine gap.
